The negative fixture answers Kyoto to “Capital of Japan?” against the reference Tokyo. The report identifies the failed case and preserves its input, output and reference.

Scope matters. Two synthetic cases demonstrate the mechanics. They do not establish application safety, evaluator quality or statistically reliable real-agent improvement. Refresh outputs from your own application, review expectations, and choose representative data and appropriate checks before relying on the gate.

Reproduce the included package

cd examples/launch-demo
bash scripts/setup.sh
.venv/bin/python scripts/run_demo.py

The verification script expects the negative control to fail. For a production gate, run evalsi run directly, as shown in the CI tutorial. The hosted Actions recipe still needs execution in your own repository.
